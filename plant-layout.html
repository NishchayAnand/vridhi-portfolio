Overview

A sustainable textile research project exploring natural dyes from kitchen waste (onion skins, avocado pits) and local plants. Documented dyeing processes, tested color fastness with various mordants, and created a library of eco-friendly color recipes.

Research Focus

The project focused on color fastness testing, mordant alternatives, and waste reduction. Results showed comparable colorfastness to synthetic dyes with zero chemical waste, demonstrating the viability of natural dyes for sustainable textile production.

Key Findings

  • → Natural dyes from kitchen waste and local plants
  • → Comparable colorfastness to synthetic alternatives
  • → Zero chemical waste production
  • → Documented dyeing processes and recipes
  • → Tested various mordant alternatives
