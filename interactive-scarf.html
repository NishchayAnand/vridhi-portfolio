Interactive Scarf

2024 • E-Textiles

Overview

A stylish scarf with embedded capacitive touch sensors woven with conductive thread. Users can tap or swipe the fabric to control music playback on their smartphone via Bluetooth.

Technical Details

Features a removable electronics module for washing, rechargeable battery, and three touch zones for play/pause, volume, and skip controls. The design seamlessly integrates technology into fashion while maintaining washability and comfort.

Key Features

  • → Capacitive touch sensors woven with conductive thread
  • → Bluetooth music playback control
  • → Removable electronics module for washing
  • → Rechargeable battery system
  • → Three touch zones (play/pause, volume, skip)
