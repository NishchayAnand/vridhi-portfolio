Textile Internship

@@ During my internship at Vardhman Textiles, I gained hands-on exposure to large-scale textile manufacturing. >>> Richa Co. & Exports /

— Apparel Internship

Jun 2025 – Aug 2025

Gurugram, Haryana

@@ During my apparel internship at Richa Co. & Exports, I was involved in the complete lifecycle of garment manufacturing—from sampling to production to merchandising. I assisted the sampling team in preparing tech packs, coordinating sample approvals, and maintaining sample records. I gained practical insights into pattern development, fit correction, BOM preparation, and fabric/trim sourcing.

@@ I also worked with the merchandising department, where I updated order sheets, tracked production progress, supported documentation, and communicated with vendors. Being on the production floor gave me a deeper understanding of stitching, finishing, and quality checkpoints. This internship strengthened my understanding of apparel workflows, timelines, cost considerations, and cross-team coordination within an export house.

Apparel Merchandising Garment Production Sourcing
